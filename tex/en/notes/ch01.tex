% DOCX paragraph 2012
\item[1.]\hypertarget{note:01-01}{}The radioactivity of our bodies is something unfairly
ignored by most. Since we are continuously exposed to gases, liquids, and solids that
are naturally radioactive, when we consume these substances, we too are radioactive.
Radioactivity is not dangerous in itself, inasmuch as there are naturally radioactive
elements with which we are often in contact. Of course, radioactivity can become
hazardous if its levels are too high. From this point of view, there is no reason to
worry as our radioactivity is so modest it is not dangerous to us or to those around us.

\end{description}
